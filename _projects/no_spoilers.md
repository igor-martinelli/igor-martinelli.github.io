### Overview
**No Spoilers** is our game about a carrot fighting its way out of a rotting fridge, the contents of which were forgotten when its owner went on a long vacation. Times were hard already, but when the power went out everything got even worse for our brave fighting carrot! Fight your way through progressively larger levels, trying to find the exit that leads to the next level. On your way you will have to kick all kinds of rotting fruits and veggies that used to be your friends, such as bananas, mushrooms or watermelons!
---

### Key Technical Features

* **Custom Game Architecture**: Built using MonoGame and C# with custom player controller mechanics, state machines, and modular item systems.
* **Procedural World Generation & Dynamic Gameplay**: Built a custom procedural level generation engine that intelligently expands map complexity and layout scale with every level cleared. Integrates fluid player movement mechanics—including sprinting, sliding, dodging, and kicking, alongside dynamic food-themed power-ups that alter weaponry in real time to counter diverse swarms of rotten fruits and veggies.
* **Team Collaboration**: Developed collaboratively using agile software engineering principles, version control workflows, and rapid prototyping cycles.
